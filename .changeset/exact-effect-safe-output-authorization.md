Add exact-effect (`resolved_effect`) safe-output authorization for `create-issue`, `update-issue`, `add-labels`, `remove-labels`, `replace-label`, `create-pull-request`, `push-to-pull-request-branch` and `merge-pull-request`. On their native manager and per-Claim routes, a protected invocation freezes its finite effect plan (REST requests, allowlisted GraphQL mutations and leased git ref updates, with follow-ups bound to typed receipts of earlier effects), obtains one authority decision bound to the plan digest, revalidates mutable preconditions, and sends only the approved effects through verifying one-shot transports. Breaking change for experimental authorization: protected outputs now reject configuration whose effects cannot be planned (`close-older-*`, `group`, `group-by-day`, `create-if-missing`, explicit fallbacks and `protected-files: fallback-to-issue`), and resolved-effect routes no longer perform fallback issues or pull requests, activation-comment links or CI-trigger commits. Custom scripts, actions and jobs remain `invocation`-bound. `gh aw validate --authorization-policy` route entries gain `effect_adapter` and `scope_binding`. Adds a bounded TLA+ model in `specs/safe-output-authorization`.
