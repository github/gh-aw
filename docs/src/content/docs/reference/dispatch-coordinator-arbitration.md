---
title: Dispatch Coordinator Claim arbitration
description: Deterministic arbitration contract for competing Dispatch Coordinator Claims
---

The Dispatch Coordinator arbitration primitive is implemented in `pkg/dispatchcoordinator`. The workflow tool, MCP commands, and runtime handoff are **not yet available**; this page describes the underlying Claim selection contract for their implementation.

For one Work ID, replay the complete current set of immutable Claims and ClaimCancellations. Identical facts may appear more than once; conflicting facts sharing an ID are invalid. Each Claim must identify its Work, claimant, and originating workflow run. A ClaimCancellation withdraws only the Claim it names, including when the cancellation is encountered before that Claim.

Among uncancelled Claims for the Work, the **lexicographically smallest Claim ID** is effective. All other uncancelled Claims are superseded, ordered by ID. Claim IDs must be generated by trusted coordinator code, not chosen by an agent. Selection depends only on fact contents: timestamps, JSONL order, Git ordering, workflow completion order, and runner scheduling have no role.

Arbitration is a projection of the *currently visible* facts, not a lease or a reservation. A later-arriving, lower-ID Claim can change the effective Claim. Activation may admit two workers from different snapshots. Before committing a Completion or any external safe output, the trusted safe-output runtime must refresh and replay the shared state and enforce an atomic publish boundary; a losing worker's outputs must be staged. Deterministic selection alone cannot prevent two runtimes acting on different snapshots from both producing irreversible external effects.
